# 03 Shared responsibility knowledge check

Practice the concepts from lesson 03. Questions from lessons 01–05 also appear in the original 1–5 test; these are the same questions, grouped by topic.

5 questions. Questions and choices are in English. Explanations are available in English and Spanish.

**How to use this test:** open one question and choose exactly the number of answers requested. Explain your reasoning before opening an answer. Inside each question, the English answer and the Spanish explanation have separate collapsed panels; either one reveals the correct answer.

**Self-check:** award one point only when your complete selection is correct. Record uncertain guesses for review. There is no timer and your score is not saved. These questions check the readings at a foundational level; they are not an exam simulation.
