# Fundamentals 1–5 knowledge check

Check your understanding of lessons 01–05: how AWS works, global infrastructure, shared responsibility, IAM and the initial service map. These 25 original practice questions cover only those readings.

**How to use this test:** open one question, choose your answer and explain your reasoning before opening **Show answer and explanation**. Both the questions and their answers start collapsed. Choose exactly the number of answers requested.

**Self-check:** give yourself one point per question only when your full selection is correct. Record uncertain guesses for review even if you got them right. There is no timer and your score is not saved.

<li><strong>B.</strong> Both servers still share the failure risk of that AZ.</li>

## What to review next

Count your points out of 25, then check each group of five separately. A low total in one group tells you which reading to revisit.

- **21–25:** a useful first grasp. Revisit uncertain answers and explain why each distractor fails.
- **16–20:** review the groups where you missed questions, then try again without looking at the explanations.
- **0–15:** reread the relevant lessons in small sections and explain each example in your own words.

These are informal study checkpoints, not a prediction of your AWS exam score. Before moving on, try explaining one scenario from each lesson without the answer choices.
